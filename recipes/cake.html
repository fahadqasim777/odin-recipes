Choclate Cake Recipe

This is how to make a choclate cake

Chocolate cake is a delicious and popular dessert enjoyed by people of all ages. It is made with ingredients such as flour, sugar, cocoa powder, eggs, butter, and milk. Chocolate cake is often served at birthdays, parties, celebrations, and other special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 1/2 cup of cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla essence
  • A pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Prepare the oven
  2. Mix the dry ingredients
  3. Add the wet ingredients
  4. Mix the batter
  5. Pour into the pan
  6. Bake the cake
  7. Cool and decorate
