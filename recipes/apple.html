Apple pie

This is how to make an apple pie

Apple pie is a delicious homemade dessert made with fresh apples, sugar, cinnamon, butter, and a crispy pastry crust. It is perfect for family gatherings, celebrations, or as a sweet treat after a meal.

Ingredients

  • 6 medium size apple
  • 2 cups of all purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup of sugar
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • 1 teas spoon of cinnamon
  • 1 table spoon of lemon juice
  • 1/4 tablespoon of salt
  • 3 to 5 table spoon of cold water

Steps

  1. Prepare the apples
  2. Make the pastry
  3. Prepare the pie crust
  4. Add the fillings
  5. Cover the pie
  6. Bake the pie
  7. Cool and Serve
